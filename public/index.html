Questions

Didn’t find your answer? Ask us directly.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your order you get a payment link. For projects we take 50% to start and 50% at launch. Bank transfer, credit card and Bit all work.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once it’s paid for, the design, code and domain are yours. We hand over every login.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Business sites and stores come with a simple editor for text, images and products, plus a short training session.

Is hosting included?

Hosting isn’t included in the price. Add a monthly care plan from ₪49, which covers hosting, SSL and backups, or host it anywhere you like.

Can the site be in Hebrew and English?

Yes, with a language switch just like this one. Right-to-left layout is built in, not patched on.

What if I already have a website?

We can redesign it, speed it up, or just maintain it. Send us the link and we’ll tell you honestly what’s worth doing.
